Privacy Policy for StreakGuard

Effective date: October 1, 2026

StreakGuard ("the app") is a personal daily-challenge reminder app for Android. It checks whether you completed the daily coding challenge on platforms you configure (e.g. LeetCode) and notifies you at a time you choose if you have not. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in Android's private app storage. We operate no servers, so this data is never uploaded to us:

  • The platform usernames you enter (e.g. your LeetCode username).
  • Your chosen daily check time and notification preferences.
  • A history of daily checks (date, platform, and whether the challenge was marked complete).

2. Network requests

To check your challenge status, the app queries the public APIs of the platforms you enable (currently leetcode.com). These requests necessarily include the username you entered, so the platform can return your public challenge data. This is governed by that platform's own privacy policy (e.g. LeetCode's). No login credentials are ever requested or stored.

3. Permissions used and why

  • Notifications – to deliver your daily reminder.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) – to trigger the daily check at the exact time you set.
  • Run at startup – to restore your daily alarm after a device reboot.
  • Internet access – to query the platforms' public challenge APIs.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings > Apps > StreakGuard > Storage > Clear data, permanently deletes everything the app stored.

6. Children’s privacy

StreakGuard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
